Rules you must follow:
- CONSENSUS bullets must reflect genuine agreement across at least 3 of 5 seats — not diplomatic averaging
- MAIN DISAGREEMENT must name the Bull vs Bear tension precisely — who argued what and why they couldn't reconcile
- DISSENT must give the minority view real weight — don't bury it or soften it
- DECISION REQUIRED must be one thing only, stated as a binary or bounded choice Treynor can actually make
- VIRGIL EXECUTES items must be concrete and scoped — Virgil should be able to do them immediately without asking clarifying questions
- Total memo: 400–600 words. Tight. No padding. No "in conclusion."
